Qwalora Ranger — candidate handbook

An independent, hands-on technical qualification. You work real tooling against a per-candidate scenario; every result is graded by a deterministic server-side grader and bound to an Ed25519 signature. This document is the operator spec: the environments you run in, the exact submission contracts, the scoring formulas, and the isolation and signing internals. It describes the live build and nothing it does not do.

Overview

Two independent pathways, each with its own ladder, environments and pass policy:

STRATACyber-defense ladder, six boards, two phases. Phase 1 (boards 1-4) is the blue-team defensive core (SOC triage, enterprise hunt, detection engineering, incident response) over a live OpenSearch / Wazuh SIEM; clearing all four earns the standalone Qwalora STRATA Defender credential. Phase 2 (boards 5-6) is the offensive GOAD-range capstone; clearing it completes the full Qwalora Ranger certification. Sequential, objective bar 95% plus an approved report per board.
PARALLAXAI-security, submission-based. You submit a code or declarative artifact to a repo; it is executed against a sealed per-candidate fixture in an isolated grader. Per-board bar 75%. Deterministic graders for most boards; a behavioral measurement with a model judge for the prompt-injection boards.

Assessment architecture

Every board, both pathways, obeys the same four properties. They are enforced in code, not by policy.

Per-candidate seeding

Each board instance is generated from a single seed derived from the tuple (github_id, board, cohort). The GitHub id is immutable, so your seed is stable across sessions and unique across the cohort; no two candidates receive the same instance, and seeds are never reused. You never see the seed — only an opaque, non-invertible case reference derived from it. All scenario data (alerts, telemetry, evidence, corpora) is a pure function of that seed.

Answer-free grading

The grading key is re-derived from your seed on the server at submit time. It is never sent to the browser, the console, or any API response. A submission returns only a score, pass/fail against the bar, and per-item correctness flags or aggregate metrics — never the ground truth. There is no key to exfiltrate from the page.

Determinism & reproducibility

Grading is a deterministic function of your submission and your seed. The same submission against the same instance yields the same score, every time — scoring is measured, not adjudicated by opinion (the one qualitative component, the PARALLAX model judge, is pinned and runs at temperature 0, and its provenance is recorded).

Pass policy

STRATAObjective score ≥ {{ pass_bar|int }}% (machine-graded, signed) AND an approved written report (human adjudication). Both are required to clear a board.
PARALLAXPer-board composite ≥ 75%, subject to board-specific critical-failure vetoes and anti-gaming floors.

Isolation & signing internals

Console isolation (STRATA boards 1–4)

The SOC console is OpenSearch Dashboards fronted by an authenticated gateway. On launch, the platform provisions your own index and binds your identity to it:

  • Your scenario is loaded into a private per-candidate index, ranger-<cohort>-<slug>-b<board>.
  • A scoped OpenSearch security role grants read on that index pattern only, with document-level security pinned to your ranger.candidate_id. Reserved/admin roles are never attached.
  • The gateway mints a short-lived RS256 JWT server-side and injects it as the Authorization header (it never reaches your browser or a URL), and forces you into your own private OpenSearch Dashboards tenant.
  • Any read outside your index — another candidate's index, a wildcard, the global telemetry — is refused with security_exception at the engine. Wandering returns nothing.

Signing

Objective results, board clearances, rank badges, incident assessments, range attestations, Board-2 evidence records and PARALLAX submission manifests and grades are each canonicalized and signed with Ed25519. The signing public key is published at /pubkey; every signed artifact is independently re-verifiable at /verify/<handle> and /submit/verify/<id>. Promotion re-verifies stored signatures and fails closed on any mismatch.

Access & onboarding

  1. Sign in — GitHub OAuth only. Admission requires a verified GitHub email, 2FA enabled, and an account older than the configured minimum. Failing any of these blocks account creation.
  2. Approval. First sign-in creates a candidate in pending with nothing provisioned. An assessor flips you to active, which opens board 1 and the shared SOC environment.
  3. Foundation. The /me/foundation tab lists the Cisco Networking Academy track for your pathway (Junior Cybersecurity Analyst + Ethical Hacker for STRATA; security fundamentals for PARALLAX). Completion is self-attested per course and is the required grounding before ladder work.
  4. Work your board. STRATA progresses sequentially from /me; PARALLAX works from the research console and the dashboard submission card. Each board states its mission, environment, and submission contract.

SOC console — OpenSearch Dashboards

Boards 1–4 investigate in live OpenSearch Dashboards. From your board, Open the SOC console → the gateway provisions your index and lands you in Discover on your own data view. The view is non-time-based, so the entire bounded scenario is visible with no time filter to fight. Pivot on the indexed fields (for triage: @timestamp, host, signal, raw; for detection/IR: event_type, host, user, image, parent, command, dest, domain, detail). Investigation happens in Discover; grading happens in the board workspace (/me/workspace), which loads the same answer-free dataset and the submission form, autosaves a draft, and grades on submit with immediate feedback and unlimited resubmission.

BOARD 3Detection EngineerWarden
Input

Labeled threat intel (malicious telemetry tagged with its ATT&CK technique) plus a benign baseline. Detectable fields: event_type, host, user, image, parent, command, dest, domain. id and timestamp are deliberately excluded so a rule cannot overfit to a unique event.

Rule grammar (Sigma subset)

Submit a ruleset. A rule is a named detection block of one or more selections combined by a condition. A field key may carry a pipe-modifier contains|startswith|endswith (optionally |all); a bare field is case-insensitive equality; a list value is OR unless all is set; across keys in a selection, logic is AND.

submission{ "rules": [ { "name": "lsass_comsvcs_minidump", "detection": { "sel": { "image": "rundll32.exe", "command|contains|all": ["comsvcs.dll", "MiniDump"] }, "condition": "sel" } } ] } # condition supports and / or / not, parentheses, # and the aggregates "all of them" / "any of them" / "N of them".

The workspace builder emits this for you: add a rule, then conditions (field · match type · value(s)); conditions on a rule are combined all of them. A raw-JSON mode is available for full control.

Scoring

Your ruleset is compiled (no candidate code or regex is ever executed) and run against a fresh seeded holdout (several malicious variants per technique plus a large benign set). The score is the F1 of macro-recall and precision:

macro_recall = mean over techniques of (detected / total) // every technique must be covered precision = detected_malicious / (detected_malicious + benign_false_positives) score = 100 · 2·macro_recall·precision / (macro_recall + precision)

Feedback returns coverage (techniques hit / total), validation false positives, per-rule firing counts, and rule compile errors — never the holdout contents. Pass ≥ {{ pass_bar|int }}%.

PARALLAX — submission contract

No live console. You work in a platform-owned GitHub repository qwalora/<cohort>-<slug> (it never holds credentials), commit the board's artifact plus writeup.md, and submit from the dashboard. Submit-time processing:

  1. POST /submit/start provisions/ensures your repo and branch and returns the clone URL.
  2. You commit the required file(s) and writeup.md, and tick the board's checklist (required deliverables must be covered or submit returns 422 unmet).
  3. POST /submit snapshots repo HEAD, computes a content digest, runs submit-time static validation of the code against a sandbox allowlist, pins an immutable tag, and Ed25519-signs a manifest binding the exact commit SHA + digest. A near-empty repo (e.g. a README) is rejected here and can never score on a baseline.
  4. Grading runs in a separate low-privilege grader over private networking (/submit/internal/*, grader token); it holds no answer key and scores the frozen snapshot. Results poll to the card; the signed record is public at /submit/verify/<id>.

Resubmission is free and unlimited; attempts auto-increment; an unchanged HEAD is an idempotent no-op; best attempt counts.

Clearing & promotion

A board clears only when both channels are satisfied: objective_passed (score ≥ {{ pass_bar|int }}%, signed) and report_status == "approved". Submit your written report from /me (POST /me/report, autosave draft via /me/report/draft); an assessor reviews it (approve / needs_changes / reject). When both land, promotion signs a clearance claim, mints the signed rank badge, frees the range slot, and advances you to the next board. You see your objective pass immediately; report approval follows review. The dashboard "Clear board N" panel shows both channel states live.

Credentials & verification

STRATA awards two standalone, publicly verifiable credentials. Clearing the Phase 1 defensive core (boards 1-4) mints the Qwalora STRATA Defender credential — a real outcome you hold and share before the Phase 2 capstone exists for you. Clearing all six boards mints the terminal Qwalora Ranger, Certified badge and graduates you. Each carries its own public QR-XXXX-XXXX cert id. Credentials are public and signed:

/verify/<handle>No-auth credential page listing badges and clearances with per-item Ed25519 signature verification.
/verify?q=Lookup by handle or certificate id, format QR-XXXX-XXXX.
/verify/<handle>/certificateThe certificate page; certificates carry issue and expiry dates.
/badges/<handle>.json · /pubkeyMachine-readable badges and the signing public key for independent verification.

You can share your verify link, certificate, cert id, and per-board rank badges (Sentinel → Vanguard). The point is third-party verifiability: the clearance behind every claim is signed and reproducible.

Operating invariants

  • Answer-free. The grading key is re-derived server-side from your seed and never returned. There is nothing to extract from the console, workspace, or API.
  • Own-data isolation. STRATA datasets derive structurally from your session identity (no handle or seed is accepted from the client); the console confines you to your own index (403 on anything else); Board-2 evidence is read only from your authorized index; PARALLAX isolation is identity + process boundary.
  • Resubmission. STRATA objective worksheets resubmit freely to reach the bar; reports resubmit after needs_changes/reject; PARALLAX submissions are unlimited, attempt-numbered, best-attempt-counts, idempotent on unchanged HEAD.
  • Everything is signed. Objectives, clearances, badges, attestations, evidence records, and submission manifests and grades carry Ed25519 signatures; promotion re-verifies them and fails closed on any tamper.
