{{ ic('shield','ic ic-sm') }} Your assignment

Senior SOC analyst. Alerts have already been triaged for maliciousness; your task is the next analytic step: scope the incidents.

For each incident you identify
  • Group the alerts that belong to it (and only those).
  • Mark the entry point: the alert where the attacker first gained access.
  • State the technique chain in the order it occurred (ATT&CK IDs).
  • List the affected hosts and accounts.
  • Cite the evidence that links the alerts: a shared account, a shared origin, a host the attacker pivoted through. Evidence only counts when it genuinely recurs across the grouped alerts.
How it is scored

Server-side against a key derived from your seed and never disclosed. Credit is for defensible correlation: grouping accuracy, the entry point, the ordered chain, the affected entities, and evidence that actually appears in the telemetry. Lumping unrelated alerts together, or guessing labels without evidence, scores poorly. Separate campaigns must be kept separate.

Not every true positive is part of a multi-step campaign; some malicious activity stands alone and should not be forced into an incident.
