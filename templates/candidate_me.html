Start herehow a board works{{ ic('chevdown','ic ic-sm') }}

STRATA is a staged qualification: you progress from SOC analyst up to the capstone across six boards. You clear a board only by meeting both an objective standard and a reviewed written report. Grading is answer-free and server-side; there are no answers to look up.

  1. Open your board. Board {{ vm.board }} of 6 ({{ vm.role_title }}) gives you a mission brief with scope and rules of engagement. Read it first.
  2. Work the mission on your board's surface: the SOC workspace on this board, your assigned case on the hunt boards, or your isolated range on the red boards.
  3. Write your report. Submit a written analysis of what you found, how, and the evidence. A reviewer reads it.
  4. Clear the board. You clear it when your objective score reaches {{ vm.pass_bar }}% and your report is approved. That mints a signed rank badge and opens the next board.
  5. Reach the capstone. Six boards stage you from SOC analyst to the capstone; clearing all six earns the signed, independently verifiable "Qwalora Ranger, Certified" credential.
When do I submit? Submit your report once you have worked the mission. The "Clear board" panel below shows your live objective score against the {{ vm.pass_bar }}% bar and your report status, so you always know what is left.

Start herehow a board works{{ ic('chevdown','ic ic-sm') }}

Each board is an independent challenge, graded automatically the moment you submit. No one grades you by hand and no model judges your work: the score is computed from a sealed test and cryptographically signed, so it is deterministic and reproducible.

  1. Pick a board in the submission card below. Each one shows its task, its pass bar, and exactly what to submit.
  2. Provision your workspace. This creates a private GitHub repo for you, seeded with the brief, a starter file to complete, and a writeup template.
  3. Do the work in that repo. Open it on GitHub or clone it, complete the code file (for the SOC board, investigate the live Wazuh console first), write your writeup, then commit and push.
  4. Submit for grading. Come back here, tick the required checklist items, and click Submit. The grader runs your code against a sealed test and signs the result.
  5. Read your score. You will see your score against the pass bar, pass or fail, and specific feedback.
When do I submit? As soon as you have pushed your completed files and ticked the required items. Resubmitting is free: push improvements and submit again, and your best attempt counts.
